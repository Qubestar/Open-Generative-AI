# Licence notes — what is inside this kit

## The kit itself
The skill (SKILL.md, reference/, components/, scripts/, template/, the design tokens and the
synthesised sounds in sfx/) is shared free by NextPhase. Use it to make your own videos,
personal or commercial. Please don't resell it or repackage it as a paid product.

## Sounds, music, footage
- **Sound effects** in `sfx/` are generated by `scripts/synth-sfx.py` from noise, sine waves and
  envelopes, shaped to the measured length, timing and level of the sounds in the reference
  edits. No samples and no third-party audio are included. Free to use in your videos.
- **No music** is included.
- **No footage, screenshots, logos or frames** of anyone else's work are included. The style was
  measured off three public documentary-style reels; only the measurements and written shot
  descriptions are here. `template/example/` is the code and plan of a real edit; none of its
  media is included.

## Your responsibility
Anything a video uses, whether a music track, a company's film, a news photo or a clip from
someone else's video, needs your rights to it (or a fair-use / commentary case you're
comfortable with). The skill writes every source and its licence into each video's `PLAN.md` and
marks unclear ones as FLAG before it renders. The final call is yours.
